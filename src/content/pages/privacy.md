This policy covers www.asgerami.com, the personal site of Amir Aman. The short
version: the site has no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers, and
I do not collect personal information through it.

## What the site stores

- **Theme preference.** If you switch between light and dark mode, the choice is
  saved in your own browser's local storage under the key `theme`, so the site
  remembers it next time. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser
  data removes it.
- **Nothing else.** There are no cookies, no account, no sign-in, no newsletter and
  no forms that send data anywhere.

## What the hosting provider logs

The site is hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). Like any
web host, Vercel processes standard request data (your IP address, browser user
agent, the page requested and the time) to deliver the site, keep it secure and
prevent abuse. I do not use this data to identify or profile visitors.

## Third parties

Fonts, images and scripts are served from this site itself, so loading a page does
not contact Google Fonts, analytics services or ad networks. Links to GitHub, X,
LinkedIn, Telegram and project sites only take you there when you click them; those
sites have their own privacy policies.

## Machine access

The site also offers machine-readable versions for AI assistants and other
software: Markdown copies of pages, [/llms.txt](/llms.txt), a JSON index and an MCP
endpoint at /api/mcp. Requests to them are handled like any other page request and
are not stored by me.

## If you email me

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply. I do not add you to
a mailing list or share your details with anyone.
