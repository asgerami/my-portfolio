Hours & Pay (on Google Play as "Hours Tracker: Time Card & Pay", and "Calculadora de Horas
y Sueldo" in Spanish) tracks shifts, breaks, tips and overtime across several jobs and
works out the pay. Everything is stored on the device. Time cards export as PDF, data as
CSV, and backups go wherever the user sends them through Android's share sheet.
