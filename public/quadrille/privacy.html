Quadrille privacy policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Quadrille is a daily puzzle app published by Amir Aman (asgerami.com). This policy explains what the Android app collects and why.

What the app stores on your device

Game progress stays on your phone: streak, stats, saved boards, reminder time, theme, and whether you have seen the first-run lessons. That data is written to local storage (AsyncStorage). It is not uploaded to a Quadrille server. There is no account and no sign-in.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification. The reminder time is stored on the device. You can turn it off in Settings.

Advertising

Quadrille is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a rewarded video for extra hints, an interstitial after some solves, and an app-open ad after the app has been in the background. Google may collect a resettable advertising ID, device and app information, and approximate location derived from IP address, and use that to show and measure ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, and some US states, a consent form may appear before personalized ads. You can also reset or opt out of the advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).

Google’s advertising policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not run our own analytics backend. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Quadrille is intended for a general audience, not specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
